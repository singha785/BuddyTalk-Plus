6. AI-Powered Lessons & Pronunciation Practice

The Lessons section should not be a simple reading module. I want it to include AI-powered pronunciation coaching so users can improve their English speaking skills by practicing with real-time feedback.

Required Features
Each lesson should contain words, phrases, and complete sentences.
The AI should first pronounce the word or sentence using a natural human-like voice (Text-to-Speech).
After listening, the user should repeat the same word or sentence.
The application should capture the user's voice using Speech-to-Text.
AI should compare the user's pronunciation with the expected pronunciation.
The system should identify pronunciation mistakes and provide detailed feedback.
If the pronunciation is incorrect, the AI should highlight the specific words or sounds that need improvement.
The AI should encourage the user to repeat the sentence until the pronunciation reaches an acceptable accuracy level.
The lesson should only move to the next word or sentence after successful pronunciation.
Display a pronunciation score (for example, 0–100%) for every attempt.
Maintain a history of pronunciation scores so users can track their improvement over time.
The AI should sound natural, friendly, and supportive while guiding the user.
AI Capabilities

The pronunciation coach should be capable of:

Detecting incorrect pronunciation.
Detecting missing or skipped words.
Detecting extra words spoken by the user.
Identifying incorrect stress and intonation wherever possible.
Providing pronunciation tips to improve fluency.
Allowing unlimited practice attempts before moving to the next lesson.
Lesson Completion Rules

A lesson should not be marked as completed simply because the user opened it.

Instead:

Every sentence in the lesson must be practiced.
The AI must verify pronunciation accuracy.
The lesson should only be marked as completed after all required sentences have been pronounced successfully.
Expected User Experience

The Lessons section should function like an intelligent English-speaking coach rather than a simple lesson viewer. It should continuously listen, evaluate pronunciation, provide corrections, and guide the user until they can pronounce each word and sentence clearly and confidently.

Additional Requirement

Please integrate a reliable AI speech technology (such as OpenAI Realtime API, Azure AI Speech, Google Cloud Speech, or another production-ready speech service) that supports:

Natural Text-to-Speech (TTS)
Accurate Speech-to-Text (STT)
Real-time pronunciation assessment
Low-latency voice interaction
High accuracy for Indian English as well as other common English accents

The pronunciation feedback should be fast, accurate, and suitable for real-time English speaking practice.
